I'm Alex Kovtunov, Director of Engineering at [Transect](https://transect.com).
Transect is a B2B SaaS site-intelligence platform that helps clean energy and
infrastructure developers find viable land and assess environmental and
permitting risk early — so projects move faster and with fewer surprises. I lead
the engineering team that builds it.

My path into this work started in code. For years I was heads-down as a
software engineer — Ruby and Rails, JavaScript, and a steady stream of side
projects (some of which you'll find in the [writing]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }})
here). Over time my focus widened from writing software to leading the people
and teams who ship it, and eventually into engineering leadership. This site is
where I write about both: the hands-on technical work, and the harder, more
human parts of building software with a team.

## What I care about

On the **engineering** side, I like pragmatic, well-shipped software: choosing
the right level of complexity, keeping systems maintainable, and using new tools
(including AI) where they genuinely reduce ambiguity and speed the team up.

On the **leadership** side, I think a lot about the connective tissue of building
products — alignment between product and engineering, a shared design system and
clear implementation patterns, and removing blockers so the team can do its best
work. Sometimes that means stepping outside your usual lane just long enough to
create a better path forward for everyone.

## Now

At Transect I lead engineering on a platform that's helping accelerate the clean
energy transition — mapping the environmental, permitting, transmission, and land
data that developers need to make fast, informed siting decisions. It's work with
real-world impact, which makes shipping it especially rewarding.

## Background

Before leading teams, I built things — web applications in Ruby on Rails,
experiments in game development and 3D graphics, a React Native app, and voice
assistant integrations. I studied at Universität Koblenz-Landau and am based in
California.
